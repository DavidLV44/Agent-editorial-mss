## What new AI-discovered drug candidates entered clinical trials recently?

### Takeaway
By October 2026, more than 200 AI-designed drugs are in active clinical development across 63+ companies, with Phase I success rates running 80–90% — roughly double the historical average. Several programs advanced to pivotal Phase III trials in mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
- As of 2026, 117 AI-enabled therapeutic assets across 63 companies have entered interventional human trials; 60 (51.3%) completed Phase I and 8 (6.8%) completed Phase II — [intuitionlabs.ai – AI-Discovered Drugs in Clinical Trials 2026](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026)
- **Rentosertib (ISM001-055)** by Insilico Medicine: TNIK inhibitor for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF); Phase III study registered July 7, 2026; Phase 2a showed +98.4 mL forced vital capacity improvement vs. -20.3 mL on placebo over 12 weeks — [intuitionlabs.ai – AI-Discovered Drugs in Clinical Trials 2026](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026); [GeneOnline – 2026 TechBio Stress Test](https://www.geneonline.com/illustrations-2026-techbio-stress-test-can-ai-deliver-real-drugs-as-recursion-insilico-medicine-and-schrodinger-take-center-stage-007/)
- **ISM6331** by Insilico Medicine: Phase I for advanced mesothelioma, FDA Fast Track status granted July 29, 2026 — [intuitionlabs.ai – AI-Discovered Drugs in Clinical Trials 2026](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026)
- **ISM0387** by Insilico Medicine / Emirates Drug Establishment (UAE): PRMT5 inhibitor targeting MTAP-deleted solid tumors including aggressive brain cancers; first fully AI-discovered drug candidate in UAE; developed with Chemistry42 platform in under 12 months; announced April 23, 2026 — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/health/emirates-drug-establishment-announces-first-fully-ai-discovered-developed-drug-candidate-1.500516693); [WAM – Emirates News Agency](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bzv0oad-emirates-drug-establishment-announces-first-fully); [EurekAlert](https://sciencesources.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1125734)
- As of October 4, 2026, UAE scientists announced AI-based discovery of a potential blood cancer drug — [The National News](https://thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/10/04/uae-scientists-harness-ai-to-discover-potential-blood-cancer-drug) *(full article not accessible; headline confirmed)*
- **REC-4881** by Recursion Pharmaceuticals: MEK1/2 inhibitor in Phase 2 (TUPELO study) for familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP); 75% of evaluable patients showed reduction in total polyp burden; 43% median reduction after 12 weeks; FDA registrational trial discussions underway — [SEC 8-K Filing Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001601830/000160183026000097/exhibit991-q0226.htm)

- **ABS-101** by AbSci: first AI-designed biologic for inflammatory bowel disease to enter Phase 1 trial; company priced a $100M equity offering led by Eli Lilly ($40M committed) — [intuitionlabs.ai](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026); [GeneOnline](https://www.geneonline.com/illustrations-2026-techbio-stress-test-can-ai-deliver-real-drugs-as-recursion-insilico-medicine-and-schrodinger-take-center-stage-007/)
- **SGR-1505** by Schrödinger: 22% overall response rate in lymphoma trial — [intuitionlabs.ai](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026)
- **GB-0895** by Generate:Biomedicines: moving to Phase 3 for severe asthma — [intuitionlabs.ai](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026)
- **Isomorphic Labs** (DeepMind spinout): Phase I starts disclosed for programs in oncology and immunology, both molecules designed in under 18 months; as of a May 2026 status report, no molecule had yet entered a human trial — [intuitionlabs.ai – Isomorphic Labs & AlphaFold](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/isomorphic-labs-alphafold-ai-drug-discovery-trials); [remio.ai – AI in Drug Discovery](https://www.remio.ai/post/ai-in-drug-discovery-how-isomorphic-labs-recursion-and-others-are-reshaping-pharma)

- Industry estimate: 15–20 AI programs may enter pivotal Phase III trials in 2026 — [intuitionlabs.ai](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026) *(projection, not confirmed fact)*

### Inferences
- Insilico Medicine is the clear leader with multiple programs in Phase II/III and a newly advanced Phase III candidate (Rentosertib), providing the first rigorous test of whether AI-designed drugs can succeed at late-stage trials.
- AbSci's capital raise backed by Eli Lilly signals major pharma confidence in AI-designed biologics as an asset class.

- **Eli Lilly + Insilico Medicine**: $2.75 billion expanded AI research collaboration — [intuitionlabs.ai – Big Pharma Hyperscaler AI Deals Tracker 2026](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/big-pharma-hyperscaler-ai-deals-tracker-2026) *(source is an aggregator; primary Lilly/Insilico press release not independently verified)*
- **Takeda + Iambic Therapeutics**: Deal worth more than $1.7 billion granting Takeda access to Iambic's AI-driven discovery and development platform — [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/takeda-iambic-ai-drug-discovery-deal-biotech/811643/) *(URL blocked; headline and summary confirmed via search snippet)*

## What AI tools are being used to improve clinical trial design, recruitment, or analysis?

### Takeaway
In 2026, purpose-built agentic AI platforms have moved beyond single-function tools to end-to-end trial lifecycle platforms, with leaders like ConcertAI, Clinsphere, and PhaseV reporting 25–50% reductions in key trial timelines and claiming significant cost savings.

### Cited Findings
- **ConcertAI ACT (Accelerated Clinical Trials)**: Enterprise agentic AI platform launched February 3, 2026; integrates real-world and proprietary data; deploys AI agents for literature review, protocol design, competitive analysis, feasibility, site selection, and patient matching; claims 10–20 months shorter trial timelines and 50% reduction in protocol design timelines and costly amendments; site selection/activation/recruitment timelines reduced by 25–50% — [hlth.com – ConcertAI ACT Launch](https://hlth.com/insights/news/concertai-launches-agentic-ai-to-accelerate-clinical-trials-2026-02-03)
- **Clinsphere Trial IntelX**: Predictive intelligence platform using WCG's benchmarked dataset of 80,000+ protocols, 40,000+ trials, and 125+ operational metrics to optimize clinical trial design before launch — [appliedclinicaltrialsonline.com – Clinsphere Trial IntelX](https://www.appliedclinicaltrialsonline.com/view/clinsphere-trial-intel-x-redefining-clinical-trial-design)
- **Trialogix**: New launch in 2026 with NCIdea Foundation grant funding; AI-powered protocol optimization using data analysis, predictive modeling, and scenario planning — [biospace.com – Trialogix Launch](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/trialogix-launches-to-transform-clinical-trial-design-with-ai-powered-optimization-platform)
- **PhaseV Trial Optimizer**: AI/ML decision support for Phase 1–3 design; claims 40% reduction in patient recruitment and trial duration, 50% cost reduction, 30%+ increase in probability of success — [medicover-mics.com – AI Tools for Clinical Trials](https://medicover-mics.com/ai-in-clinical-trials/ai-tools-for-clinical-trials/)
- **Dyania Health**: AI-powered patient recruitment from EHRs; identifies eligible candidates in minutes vs. hours of manual review; 96% accuracy in patient matching — [dataally.ai – Best Clinical Trial Recruitment Software](https://www.dataally.ai/blog/best-clinical-trial-recruitment-software)
- **Tempus/Deep 6 AI, ConcertAI, Mendel**: EHR-based matching engines for patient identification — [rework.com – Best AI Tools for Clinical Trial Recruitment 2026](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-clinical-trial-recruitment-2026)
- **Trial Pathfinder** (Stanford / James Zou): AI tool that optimizes eligibility criteria to double recruitment rates without impacting safety outcomes — [AHA Market Scan – How AI Is Transforming Clinical Trials](https://www.aha.org/aha-center-health-innovation-market-scan/2025-10-21-how-ai-transforming-clinical-trials)
- AI-powered patient recruitment tools can improve enrollment rates by up to 65%; predictive analytics models achieve 85% accuracy in forecasting trial outcomes — [kitsa.ai – AI Transforming Clinical Trials 2026](https://kitsa.ai/blog/ai-transforming-clinical-trials) *(these are vendor/aggregator claims, not independently verified trial results)*
- SCOPE 2026 Conference (February 2026): Industry consensus that AI in clinical research is "poised for boom times" — [clinicalresearchnewsonline.com – SCOPE 2026](https://www.clinicalresearchnewsonline.com/news/2026/02/03/scope-2026--ai-in-clinical-research-poised-for-boom-times)
- Nearly 80% of trials miss their original enrollment timeline; AI tools are increasingly applied to address this structural problem — [rework.com](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-clinical-trial-recruitment-2026)
- FHIR-connected EHR screening tools can apply trial eligibility criteria programmatically against structured patient records, surfacing candidates missed by manual chart review — [appliedclinicaltrialsonline.com – AI-Driven Clinical Trial Recruitment](https://www.appliedclinicaltrialsonline.com/view/ai-driven-clinical-trial-recruitment-design)

### Inferences
- The shift from point solutions (single-function patient matching) to end-to-end agentic platforms (ConcertAI ACT) mirrors the broader pharma trend toward integrated AI systems rather than narrow tools.
- The combination of FHIR interoperability standards with AI eligibility screening represents a structural infrastructure improvement that could compound the recruitment gains beyond what any standalone tool achieves.

### Gaps
- ClinicalTrials.gov registry was not directly searched for October 2026 IND filings due to tool access limitations; newly registered AI-designed drug trials from the specific September–October 2026 window may be missing.
- Performance claims from most clinical trial AI vendors (enrollment +65%, cost -50%) are self-reported by vendors, not from peer-reviewed studies; independent validation literature was not located for the October 2026 timeframe.
- Specific European CRO or hospital-based clinical trial AI deployments from the target date range were not identified in accessible sources.
