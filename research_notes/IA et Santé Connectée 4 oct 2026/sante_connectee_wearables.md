## Are there new remote patient monitoring programs adopted by health systems?

### Takeaway
The CMS ACCESS Model, launched in July 2026 as a 10-year nationwide program, is the single most significant structural adoption event of the year, with over 150 health organizations enrolled by its launch and direct financial incentives tied to patient outcomes.

### Cited Findings
- In July 2026, CMS launched the ACCESS Model, a 10-year initiative (July 5, 2026–June 30, 2036) supporting technology-enabled chronic care for Medicare beneficiaries including remote monitoring and wearables; more than 150 healthcare organizations enrolled by July 20, 2026; beneficiary copays for ACCESS-covered services are waived — [Validic/CMS ACCESS Overview](https://www.validic.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/access-program/index.html); [MSMS](https://www.msms.org/news/cms-launches-access-model-expand-tech-enabled-chronic-care); [AFS Law ACCESS Update](https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/health-care-counsel-blog/cms-access-model-update-payment-rates-and-performance-targets)
- ACCESS covers chronic conditions including hypertension, diabetes, heart failure, chronic MSK pain, depression/anxiety, and cardio-metabolic conditions; the standard comanagement payment is $30 per service; Withings Medical was announced as a participating provider — [Foley](https://www.foley.com/p/102lzg9/cms-access-model-providers-prepare-for-technology-driven-value-based-care/)
- RPM adoption among US physicians rose from 13% in 2018 to over 38% in 2025; hospital-at-home programs combine RPM, wearables, AI, and telehealth for acute care delivery — [Tenovi RPM Guide](https://www.tenovi.com/remote-patient-monitoring/); [JMIR hospital-at-home study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12021374/)
- A CMS Innovation Center study demonstrated 25–40% reductions in hospital readmissions for heart failure patients using home-health remote monitoring — [OpenPR Market Outlook](https://www.openpr.com/news/4603120/remote-patient-monitoring-market-outlook-2026-highlights)
- The global RPM market is valued at approximately US$67.3 billion in 2026 and projected to reach US$117.9 billion by 2033 — [Yahoo Finance/Persistence Market Research](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/healthcare/articles/remote-patient-monitoring-market-reach-141500539.html)
- Healthcare-at-home programs are characterized as a "structural shift" in how care is delivered across hospitals, nursing homes, corporate health programs, and insurers as of 2026 — [Smartfuture](https://www.oursmartfuture.com/healthcare-at-home-2026-what-matters-most-when-choosing-remote-patient-monitoring-solutions/)

### Inferences
- The CMS ACCESS Model creates a sustained reimbursement mechanism that should significantly accelerate RPM adoption beyond the current 38% physician rate over the next several years.
- Key barriers persist: payor policy alignment, data security, interoperability, and cultural adoption among both clinicians and patients.

### Gaps
- No specific new named health system (e.g., Mayo Clinic, Kaiser) adoption announcements for RPM from September–October 2026 were found in the sources; most coverage is about the broader market and CMS policy, not individual health-system rollouts.

## What advances exist in continuous monitoring (glucose, cardiac, sleep, etc.)?

### Takeaway
CGM technology has moved decisively into the mainstream with over-the-counter availability (Dexcom Stelo, Abbott Lingo), while cardiac and sleep monitoring gained new FDA-cleared wearable patches and Google debuted predictive AFib detection in patent filings.

### Cited Findings
- **Glucose:** Dexcom Stelo and Abbott Lingo launched in the US as the first FDA-cleared over-the-counter CGMs (available on Amazon), intended for non-insulin users (non-diabetics and Type 2 without insulin); Dexcom is transitioning fully to the G7 (15.5-day wear, 30-min warm-up), ceasing G6 manufacture after July 1, 2026; Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3 Plus is the world's smallest/thinnest CGM; Abbott also announced Libre Rio as a second OTC sensor — [MedTech Dive OTC CGM](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/dexcom-abbott-otc-cgms-diabetes-tech/726715/); [MedTech Dive launch details](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/abbott-dexcom-over-the-counter-cgm-launch/719928/); [Drug Delivery Business – G6 discontinuation](https://www.drugdeliverybusiness.com/dexcom-cease-g6-cgm-production-2026/)
- **Cardiac/AFib:** Google filed a patent (reported October 3, 2026) for a smartwatch system that learns from irregular heartbeat patterns and predicts when a future AFib episode may occur — [Gadgets & Wearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/10/03/google-smartwatch-predict-afib-patent)
- **Cardiac/Blood Pressure:** The FDA cleared an Apple Watch algorithm for hypertension detection — [Cardiovascular Business](https://cardiovascularbusiness.com/topics/clinical/hypertension/fda-clears-apple-watch-algorithm-detecting-hypertension); Nanowear's SimpleSense-Wrist (using deep-learning interpretation of bio-impedance spectroscopy for continuous blood pressure and vascular stiffness) received FDA Breakthrough Device Designation in February 2026 — [AI-Powered Wearables Guide](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/gadgets/ai-powered-wearables-in-2026-advancing-personalized-preventive-healthcare)
- **Sleep:** Empatica EmbraceMini received FDA 510(k) clearance in January 2026 for sleep monitoring and actigraphy-based assessment of sleep and physical activity for remote clinical monitoring — [Life Science Daily – FDA Cleared Wearables](https://lifesciencedaily.news/from-wrist-to-ward-which-wearables-have-earned-clinical-trust/)
- **Sleep/Cardiac combo:** SANSA (Huxley Medical), a chest-worn sleep apnea diagnostic patch, received FDA 510(k) clearance; it measures 8 channels (SpO2, EKG-derived HR, respiratory effort, chest movement, sleep staging, snoring, body position, actigraphy) and detects AFib alongside sleep apnea with 88.2% sensitivity — [Sleep Review Magazine](https://sleepreviewmag.com/sleep-diagnostics/home-testing/home-apnea-testing/fda-clears-sansa-chest-worn-sleep-apnea-diagnostic-patch/); [AASM](https://aasm.org/fda-clears-tiptraq-an-ai-enabled-hsat/)
- **Sleep (AI-enabled HSAT):** FDA cleared TipTraQ, an AI-enabled home sleep apnea test — [AASM](https://aasm.org/fda-clears-tiptraq-an-ai-enabled-hsat/)
- Google Pixel Watch Health Guardian tracks insulin resistance trends and blood pressure trends continuously in the background — [Droid-Life](https://www.droid-life.com/2026/09/24/pixel-watchs-health-guardian-is-rolling-out-to-your-watch/)
- Wearables in 2026 increasingly include sensors for oxygen saturation, skin temperature, stress levels, and advanced biometrics — [HealthArc RPM Trends](https://www.healtharc.io/blogs/8-remote-patient-monitoring-rpm-trends/)

### Inferences
- The convergence of sleep apnea detection and cardiac monitoring (SANSA) reflects recognition that these comorbidities frequently co-occur and benefit from simultaneous monitoring.
- OTC CGM availability (Dexcom/Abbott) marks a watershed shift, extending metabolic monitoring to the general population without a prescription.

### Gaps
- No specific date confirmed for FDA clearance of the Apple Watch hypertension algorithm; the clearance is referenced but exact clearance number not found.
- SANSA clearance date: the original clearance news appears to date from late 2024, not 2026; this may not be a "new" 2026 development — this is flagged as uncertain.
